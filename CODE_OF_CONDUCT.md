# Code of Conduct

CodeCritter is a friendly little project and we want contributing to it to feel the same way.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with first-time contributors.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Spam, including unsolicited promotion in issues and pull requests.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem harmful.

To report a problem, message the maintainer privately on Instagram ([@rsd.exe](https://instagram.com/rsd.exe)) or through GitHub ([@rsd-06](https://github.com/rsd-06)). Reports are kept confidential.
